01 · Delivery approach

How I run a project

The same lifecycle sits behind every site I deliver: plan it on the ground, put it on paper, align the people, then build and track it.

  1. 01

    Survey & feasibility

    Site surveys and feasibility assessments that shape field planning and deployment.

  2. 02

    Documentation

    Project documentation and requirements that the client, vendors and teams can work from.

  3. 03

    Stakeholder alignment

    Clients, vendors, regional teams and head office, kept aligned on scope and timelines.

  4. 04

    On-site implementation

    Field execution with technical teams and vendors, with issues resolved on the ground.

  5. 05

    Monitor & report

    Progress monitoring and reporting through to delivery against the plan.
